##### **Digital Media and Intelligent Network (DMIN) Laboratory**

The Digital Media and Intelligent Network (DMIN) Laboratory focuses on multimedia information processing, network communications, video coding, computer vision, and medical image processing. Led by Professor Yu Li, the laboratory consists of three faculty members and over forty graduate students, who work together toward both academic excellence and personal growth. The diversity of the group fosters active exchanges within the lab. In addition, we regularly organize recreational activities and games, which help create a warm and welcoming atmosphere for all members.
