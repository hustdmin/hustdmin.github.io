In recent years we have improved key coding tools such as geometric partitioning, affine motion, and template matching merge (with multiple papers at DCC, PCS, and VCIP), built CNN-based fast mode decision and in-loop filtering to reduce complexity, developed perception-driven rate control (panoramic video, transform-coefficient modeling), and explored learned image/video coding for machines — balancing coding efficiency, complexity, and intelligent applications.
