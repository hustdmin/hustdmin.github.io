  The 'Puyuan Talent' Scholarship was established in 2022 by RIGOL Technologies Inc. through a donation to our university, to reward outstanding master's and doctoral students in the School of Electronic Information and Communications who have achieved prominent competition results. On September 21, the inaugural 'Puyuan Talent' Scholarship award ceremony of the School of Electronic Information and Communications was successfully held in Lecture Hall A101 of East Building 17, and our lab's student Du Boyang won the award.
  
  ![image](assets/img/news/2023-puyuan.jpg)

“普源英才”奖学金由普源精电科技股份有限公司2022年向我校捐赠设立，用于奖励电子信息与通信学院竞赛成果突出的优秀硕博学生。9月21日，电信学院首届“普源英才”奖学金颁奖仪式在东十七楼A101报告厅顺利举行，我实验室杜博阳同学获奖。
