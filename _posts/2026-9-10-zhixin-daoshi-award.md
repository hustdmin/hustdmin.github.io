  On September 10, 2026, Huazhong University of Science and Technology held its 2026 Teachers' Day commendation conference. Under this year's theme of "carrying forward the spirit of educators and fostering a culture of respecting teachers and education," the conference paid tribute to and commended outstanding faculty and staff representatives who have devoted themselves to teaching and research, taken on responsibilities in the university's development, and led by example in cultivating talent.

  Professor **Yu Li** attended the conference and received the award certificate for the university's graduate "Understanding Mentor" title on stage.

9月10日，华中科技大学举行2026年教师节表彰大会。今年教师节的主题为"大力弘扬教育家精神，共筑尊师重教风尚"。大会向深耕教学科研一线、在学校事业发展中勇担使命、在立德树人中率先垂范的优秀教职工代表致以崇高敬意并予以表彰。

实验室喻莉教授参加大会，并现场接受研究生"知心导师"称号颁奖。
