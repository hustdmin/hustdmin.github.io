  On the evening of May 31, 2024, DMIN Lab organized its May 2024 group meeting. The sharing session featured research reports by Li Bingyin and Du Boyang. First, student Li Bingyin presented on the rapid development of NeRF technology. Focusing on NeRF (Neural Radiance Fields), which won the Best Paper award at ECCV 2020, he introduced its applications in novel view synthesis and 3D reconstruction, then explained in detail few-shot NeRF and human-perception-guided view synthesis schemes, and finally demonstrated the experimental results of NeRF in the above research. Student Du Boyang shared content on improving object detection accuracy in low-light environments, mainly focusing on image enhancement and object detection under low-light conditions, proposing a solution based on generative adversarial networks and multi-exposure generation fusion algorithms, and verifying its effectiveness and advantages through qualitative and quantitative analysis. The lab's faculty and students listened carefully to the presentations and exchanged ideas, gaining a great deal.

2024年5月31日晚，DMIN实验室组织了2024年5月大组交流会。本次交流会分享由李丙银和杜博阳向大家作科研经验汇报。首先李丙银同学向大家进行了NERF技术的迅猛发展的主题分享，针对在2020年ECCV会议上获得Best Paper的NeRF（Neural Radiance Fields）介绍了其在新视点合成、三维重建等技术方向应用，然后重点讲解了小样本NREF和基于人类感知引导的视图合成方案，最终展示了NeRF在以上研究中的实验结果。杜博阳同学向大家分享了低光照环境中提高目标检测精度的主题内容，主要围绕在低光照条件下的图像增强和目标检测展开，提出了基于生成对抗网络和多曝光生成融合算法的解决方案，并通过定性和定量分析验证了其有效性和优势。实验室的老师和同学们认真听取了分享内容并进行交流，收益很多。
