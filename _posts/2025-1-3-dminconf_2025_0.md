  On the evening of January 3, 2025, at 7:00 PM, the lab's group meeting was successfully held in Conference Room A101, East Building 17. The meeting was chaired by Associate Professor Yang Peng, and covered the holiday arrangements and the thesis timelines for the class of 2025 master's and doctoral graduates, as well as academic presentations by several students.
  
  At the beginning of the meeting, Associate Professor Yang Peng gave a detailed explanation of the arrangements for the 2025 Spring Festival holiday and set out the timeline requirements for the graduation thesis progress of the class of 2025 master's and doctoral students, providing clear direction and reference to ensure sufficient preparation and planning.
  
  Subsequently, master's student Zhang Xinling and doctoral student Fan Xuanhe presented their recent work. Zhang Xinling's presentation, "Multi-Object Tracking Based on Multi-Modal Perception," detailed the research background, technical challenges, and current progress of the topic, focusing on the application and innovation of multi-modal data fusion in multi-object tracking. Her work provides new ideas for improving multi-object tracking technology and demonstrates the importance of perceptual system fusion.
  
  Following that, doctoral student Fan Xuanhe shared his research results on "Design and Follow-Up Control of Facial Robots." Fan introduced the core ideas and technical implementation of facial robot design and its application in autonomous follow-up control, combining precise control of facial robots with artificial intelligence technology to advance technical progress and application expansion in the field.
  
  The meeting concluded successfully. Participants expressed that they had benefited greatly and would continue to work hard to tackle research challenges and push the lab's research forward.

2025年1月3日晚7点，实验室大组会在东17楼A101会议室顺利举行。会议由杨鹏副教授主持，会议内容涵盖了放假安排及2025届硕博毕业生的论文时间节点，同时也安排了几位同学的学术汇报。
会议伊始，杨鹏副教授就2025年春节假期的安排进行了详细说明，并对2025届硕士和博士毕业生的毕业论文进度作出了相关时间节点要求，为毕业生们提供了明确的方向和参考，确保大家在时间上有足够的准备和规划。
随后，硕士生张鑫祾和博士生范烜赫分别进行了近期工作汇报。张鑫祾的汇报主题是“基于多种感知的多目标跟踪”，她详细介绍了该课题的研究背景、技术挑战及目前的研究进展，重点探讨了多感知数据融合在多目标跟踪中的应用与创新。张鑫祾的工作为多目标跟踪技术的提升提供了新的思路，并展现了感知系统融合的重要性。
紧接着，范烜赫博士生分享了他的研究成果，主题为“面部机器人的制作与跟随控制方法研究”。范烜赫介绍了面部机器人设计的核心思想、技术实现及其在自主跟随控制中的应用，他的研究将面部机器人的精确控制与人工智能技术相结合，推动了该领域的技术进步和应用拓展。
会议圆满结束，大家纷纷表示受益匪浅，将继续努力攻克科研难题，推动实验室研究的不断前进。
