  From October 6 to October 10, 2024, Professor Yu Li, Associate Professor Yan Zengqiang, and Ph.D. student Lin Xian were invited to attend the 27th International Conference on Medical Image Computing and Computer-Assisted Intervention (MICCAI 2024). This conference is an internationally recognized and most influential academic conference in the field of medical image analysis, focusing on the latest research and technological innovation in medical image computing and computer-assisted intervention. MICCAI 2024 was held in Marrakech, Morocco. The conference received a total of 2,879 submissions and finally accepted 857. All 4 papers submitted by DMIN Lab were accepted.

2024年10月6日至2024年10月10日，喻莉教授、闫增强副教授、林娴博士受邀参加第27届国际医学图像计算和计算机辅助干预会议（MICCAI 2024），该会议是医学图像分析领域国际公认的最具影响力的学术会议，聚焦医学影像计算和计算机辅助干预领域的最新研究和技术创新。MICCAI 2024于摩洛哥马拉喀什举办，大会共收稿2879篇，最终录取857篇，DMIN实验室投稿的4篇论文均被接收。
