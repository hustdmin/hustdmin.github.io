  Congratulations to all the students, and thanks to Professor Yu Li for her dedicated guidance and to all collaborators for their joint efforts.
  
  The information of the five accepted papers is as follows:
  
  - **Zhu Jiabao** (Oral): *Angular Weighted Prediction Mode Improvements Beyond AVS3*
  - **Zhong Yucheng** (Oral): *Adaptive Enhanced Affine Inter Mode for the Next Generation of AVS*
  - **Li Hongbo** (Oral): *Improvements on Template Matching Merge Mode for the Next Generation of AVS*
  - **Zeng Jiaxin**: *Adaptive Multi-Size CNN: Fast Mode Decision for VVC-SCC*
  - **Lai Wanglin, Ma Licong**: *Intra Prediction Mode Optimization Based on Directional Enhancement and Cross-Component Fusion*

近日，我实验室（数字媒体与智能网络实验室，DMIN Lab）共有 5 篇论文被数据压缩领域权威会议 **2026 Data Compression Conference (DCC)** 录用。其中，朱家宝、钟煜城、李鸿波同学作为第一作者的 3 篇论文被录用为**口头报告（Oral Presentation）**，曾家鑫、赖汪林同学作为第一作者的 2 篇论文亦被录用。

在此向各位同学表示祝贺，也感谢喻莉教授的悉心指导以及全体合作者的共同努力。

录用的 5 篇论文信息如下：

- **朱家宝**（Oral）：*Angular Weighted Prediction Mode Improvements Beyond AVS3*
- **钟煜城**（Oral）：*Adaptive Enhanced Affine Inter Mode for the Next Generation of AVS*
- **李鸿波**（Oral）：*Improvements on Template Matching Merge Mode for the Next Generation of AVS*
- **曾家鑫**：*Adaptive Multi-Size CNN: Fast Mode Decision for VVC-SCC*
- **赖汪林、马丽聪**：*Intra Prediction Mode Optimization Based on Directional Enhancement and Cross-Component Fusion*

Data Compression Conference (DCC) 是数据压缩领域的国际旗舰会议，汇聚了学术界与工业界在信号、图像、视频及多媒体数据压缩方向的最新研究成果。
