  To stimulate students' enthusiasm for scientific research and learning, and to strengthen their understanding of cutting-edge research in the field, on December 23, more than ten DMIN Lab master's and doctoral students, led by Professor Yu Li of the School of Electronic Information and Communications at Huazhong University of Science and Technology, were invited to visit the Human Factors Engineering Institute of Wuhan Industrial Innovation Development Research Institute for a study tour and exchange.
  
  First, Institute Director Song Yang introduced the establishment background, development history, and future plans of the Wuhan Industrial Innovation Development Research Institute (WIDIR) and the Human Factors Engineering Institute to the DMIN faculty and students. Then, Zhou Xunzong of the Human Factors Engineering Institute gave a live introduction and demonstration of a wearable multimodal data intelligent collection bracelet to the teachers and students. The bracelet looks similar to ordinary smart bracelets and can monitor a person's physiological state in real time. Unlike ordinary bracelets, it can also monitor a person's emotional changes, safeguarding the life, health, and mental health of adults and adolescents. Finally, the institute's General Manager Zhou introduced the multimodal data fusion collection platform to the students and detailed existing multimodal data collection application cases. Combining the lab's research on visual human-computer interaction, the DMIN faculty and students held an exchange seminar with the institute team members on PPG, behavior recognition, and the use of the multimodal platform.
  
  During this visit, the students learned about the application of human factors engineering from an industry perspective, and gained a deeper understanding of vision-based human-computer interaction research.

为了激发学生的科研学习热情，加强学生对专业前沿研究的了解，12月23号，DMIN实验室硕博十余名学生在华中科技大学电信学院喻莉教授带领下，应邀赴武汉产业创新发展研究院人因工程研究所进行参观访问和学习交流。

首先，研究所所长宋杨向DMIN师生介绍了武创院和人因工程研究所的设立背景与发展历程和未来规划。然后，由人因工程研究所的周勋总为老师和同学们现场介绍和演示可穿戴式多模态数据智能采集手环。手环看起来与普通智能手环类似，能够实时监测人的生理状态。与普通手环不同的是，其还能够监测人的情绪变化，为成人和青少年的生命健康和心理健康保驾护航。最后，研究所的周总为同学们介绍了多模态数据融合采集平台，并详细介绍了现有多模态数据采集应用案例。DMIN实验室师生结合实验室视觉人机交互的研究情况，针对PPG、行为识别以及多模态平台的使用情况等内容与研究所团队成员开展了交流座谈。

在此次参观中，同学们从产业角度听取了人因工程的应用情况，对基于视觉的人机交互研究也有了更加深刻的认识。
