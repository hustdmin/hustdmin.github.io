  On the evening of June 28, 2024, DMIN Lab organized its June 2024 group meeting. The sharing session featured research presentations by Qin Tian and Liang Yuxin. First, student Qin Tian presented on point cloud processing and adaptive transmission for vehicle-road collaborative perception. Targeting the problems of data redundancy and overlap in collaborative perception and edge computing, he designed an EPAT system that selectively transmits data to improve perception efficiency. Then, student Liang Yuxin presented on the deployment of generative artificial intelligence models in mobile edge networks, focusing on the deployment methods, challenges, motivations, system models, cost models, problem formulation, and optimization algorithms of generative AI models in edge computing, and demonstrated the algorithm's performance advantages in resources and latency through experimental comparisons. The lab's faculty and students listened carefully to the presentations and exchanged ideas, gaining a great deal.

2024年6月28日晚，DMIN实验室组织了2024年6月大组交流会。本次交流会分享由秦添和梁雨欣向大家进行科研内容分享。首先秦添同学向大家进行了面向⻋路协同感知的点云处理和⾃适应传输主题分享，针对在协同感知和边缘计算中存在的数据冗余和重叠的问题，设计了一种可以进行选择性地传输数据以提高感知效率的EPAT系统。然后梁雨欣同学作了主题为移动边缘⽹络中的⽣成式⼈⼯智能模型的部署的科研内容分享，重点讨论了生成式AI模型在边缘计算中的部署方法、面临的挑战、动机、系统模型、代价模型、问题表述及优化算法，并通过实验比较展示了算法在资源和延迟上的性能优势。实验室的老师和同学们认真听取了分享内容并进行交流，收益很多。
