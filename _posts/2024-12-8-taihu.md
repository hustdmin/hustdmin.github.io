  On December 8, 2024, the School of Future Technology successfully held an event titled 'Linking the Future, Sailing with Excellence — 2024 Future Science and Innovation Academic Forum and Undergraduate Science and Technology Innovation Achievement Exhibition' at the Optoelectronic Information Building of Huazhong University of Science and Technology. The event comprehensively showcased the school's latest achievements in enhancing students' comprehensive literacy, promoting breakthroughs in science and technology innovation, and exploring practical applications.
  
  At the event, important guests attended, including You Zheng, President of Huazhong University of Science and Technology; Academician Ding Han, Director of the University Academic Committee and Dean of the School of Future Technology; Feng Dan, Vice President of the University; Zhang Tao, Deputy Secretary of the University Party Committee and Secretary of the Party Committee of the School of Optoelectronic Information; and guest professor Partha Pratim Banerjee. The forum was hosted by Tang Ming, Executive Dean of the School of Future Technology, and attracted many experts and scholars from academia and industry, who jointly witnessed the school's achievements in innovative education and the transformation of scientific research results.
  
  As an important supporting fund within the lab, the **Taihu Fund** has been committed to promoting science and technology innovation and talent cultivation, especially playing a positive role in strengthening the integration of industry, academia, and research. At this launch event, Gao Qianwen, a master's student of the lab, attended as one of the representatives of the fund. During the conference, by listening to the wonderful reports of the participating guests, she gained an in-depth understanding of the latest developments of the School of Future Technology in science and technology innovation and future technology fields. Although Gao Qianwen did not give a report at the meeting, she gained a lot of valuable academic and practical experience through interactive exchanges with the experts present.
  
  The Taihu Fund is a fund established within our lab dedicated to science and technology innovation and talent cultivation, aiming to provide scientific research support and resources for lab members and promote academic exchanges and cooperation. Through this event, the Taihu Fund further supported the in-depth cooperation between academia and industry, and provided lab members with more opportunities to engage in dialogue with frontier technology and innovative research.
  
  In the future, the Taihu Fund will continue to increase its support for science and technology innovation and academic exchanges, helping the lab's sustained development in the field of scientific research, and making more contributions to the country's scientific and technological progress and innovation.

2024年12月8日，未来技术学院在华中科技大学光电信息大楼成功举办了主题为“链接未来·卓越启航——2024未来科创学术论坛暨本科生科技创新成果展”的活动。此次活动全面展示了学院在提升学子综合素养、推动科技创新突破及实践探索应用方面的最新成果。

活动现场，华中科技大学校长尤政、校学术委员会主任兼未来技术学院院长丁汉院士、校副校长冯丹、校党委副书记兼光电信息学院党委书记张涛以及客座教授Partha Pratim Banerjee等重要嘉宾出席。论坛由未来技术学院执行院长唐明主持，吸引了众多学术界和产业界的专家学者，共同见证了学院在创新教育和科研成果转化方面的成就。

作为实验室中的重要支持基金，**太湖基金**一直致力于推动科技创新和人才培养，尤其是在加强产学研结合方面发挥了积极作用。此次发布会中，实验室硕士生高倩文作为基金的代表之一出席了活动。在会议期间，她通过聆听与会嘉宾的精彩报告，深入了解了未来技术学院在科技创新和未来技术领域的最新发展。虽然高倩文并未在会上进行汇报，但她通过与与会专家的互动交流，收获了许多有价值的学术与实践经验。

太湖基金是我们实验室内部设立的一项专注于科技创新和人才培养的基金，旨在为实验室成员提供科研支持与资源，推动学术交流与合作。通过此次活动，太湖基金进一步支持了学术界与产业界的深度合作，并为实验室成员提供了更多与前沿科技和创新研究对话的机会。

未来，太湖基金将继续加大对科技创新和学术交流的支持力度，助力实验室在科研领域的持续发展，并为国家的科技进步与创新做出更多贡献。
