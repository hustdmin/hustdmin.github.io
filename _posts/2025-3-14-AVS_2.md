  From March 12 to 15, 2025, the 92nd meeting of the AVS Working Group was held in Changsha. The lab's coding group submitted three proposals: "Decoder-Side Non-Translational Affine Parameter Optimization for Affine Coding Blocks" by Zhong Yucheng, "ETMVP Candidate Motion Vector Optimization Based on Template Matching" by Li Hongbo, and "Improvement of Candidate Motion Vector List for Angular Weighted Prediction Mode" by Zhu Jiabao. All were reviewed and adopted by the AVS Video Group into the latest AVS4 reference software EVM platform.
  
  The Audio Video Coding Standard (AVS) Working Group was established in June 2002 with the approval of the former Science and Technology Department of the Ministry of Information Industry. Its mission is to meet the needs of China's information industry, unite domestic enterprises and research institutions, and formulate (revise) common technical standards for compression, decompression, processing, and representation of digital audio and video, providing efficient and economical codec technology for digital audio and video devices and systems, serving major information industry applications such as high-resolution digital broadcasting, high-density laser digital storage media, wireless broadband multimedia communications, and Internet broadband streaming media.
  
  The first-generation AVS video coding standard was finalized in December 2003, with compression efficiency comparable to the contemporary international standard MPEG-2. Subsequently, an enhanced version AVS+ was proposed based on AVS and finalized in May 2012, with compression efficiency comparable to the contemporary international standard AVC/H.264. The second-generation AVS video coding standard (AVS2) was finalized in 2014, with compression efficiency comparable to the international standard H.265/HEVC. The third-generation AVS video coding standard (AVS3) began development in 2017, mainly targeting 8K ultra-high definition, and completed standardization in June 2021, with compression efficiency benchmarked against H.266/VVC.
  
  Based on the development of technologies and applications such as UHD/HDR video, 3D video, panoramic video, VR/AR, and production domains, the fourth-generation AVS video coding standard (AVS4) officially released its requirements document in June 2023 to meet the needs of the future video industry. Currently, our lab's coding group is participating in the development of AVS4 codec standard technologies.

2025年3月12日-15日，AVS工作组第九十二次会议在长沙召开。实验室编码组共递交3份提案，分别是钟煜城同学的提案“仿射编码块的解码端非平移仿射参数优化”、李鸿波同学的提案“基于模板匹配的ETMVP候选运动矢量优化“以及朱家宝同学的“角度加权预测模式候选运动矢量列表改进”，均被AVS视频组审议通过并采纳到最新的AVS4参考软件EVM平台中。

数字音视频编解码技术标准（Audio Video coding Standard, AVS）工作组由国家原信息产业部科学技术司于2002年6月批准成立。工作组的任务是：面向我国的信息产业需求，联合国内企业和科研机构，制（修）订数字音视频的压缩、解压缩、处理和表示等共性技术标准，为数字音视频设备与系统提供高效经济的编解码技术，服务于高分辨率数字广播、高密度激光数字存储媒体、无线宽带多媒体通讯、互联网宽带流媒体等重大信息产业应用。

第一代AVS视频编码标准在2003年12月定稿，压缩效率与同期国际标准MPEG-2相当。后续在AVS的基础上提出了增强版本AVS+，在2012年5月定稿，压缩效率与同期国际标准AVC/H.264相当。第二代AVS视频编码标准（AVS2）于2014年定稿，压缩效率与国际标准H.265/HEVC相当。第三代AVS视频编码标准（AVS3）自2017年开始制定，主要面向8K超高清，并于2021年6月完成标准化，压缩效率对标H.266/VVC。

根据UHD/HDR视频、3D视频、全景视频、VR/AR以及制作域等技术与应用的发展情况，第四代AVS视频编码标准（AVS4）已于2023年6月正式发布需求文档，以满足未来视频产业的需要。目前我实验室编码组正在参与AVS4编解码标准技术的制定工作。
