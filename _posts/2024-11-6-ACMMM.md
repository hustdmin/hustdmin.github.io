  In October 2024, the 32nd ACM International Conference on Multimedia (ACM MM) was grandly held in Melbourne, Australia. Experts and scholars in the multimedia field from around the world gathered to discuss the latest research progress and technological breakthroughs. Professor Yu Li of the lab and her master's student Shi Junjie were invited to attend the conference, presenting their team's latest research results in the fields of multimedia and computer vision, which received high attention and appreciation from the participating experts. For Shi Junjie, participating in the conference as both a volunteer and a speaker was an important milestone in his academic journey. He shared that attending ACM MM not only provided him with a unique platform for learning and exchange, but also brought him many valuable opportunities for personal growth. As a conference volunteer, Shi Junjie participated in the organization of the conference throughout, actively assisting the participation process, and interacting closely with organizers, staff, and attendees. The volunteer experience not only enhanced his understanding of conference operations, but also allowed him to more deeply integrate into the international research community. In the oral presentation session, Shi Junjie presented his research results to top scholars worldwide for the first time, both excited and nervous. However, in the strong academic atmosphere, he gradually turned from uneasiness to confidence. The audience raised constructive feedback, which inspired him to further reflect on his research and prompted him to continue exploring and improving in future work. He said he is very grateful to the ACM MM organizers for the valuable opportunity to present his research on the international stage and expand his academic horizons in the multimedia field. Professor Yu Li expressed appreciation for the team members' performance, believing that this ACM MM trip not only earned international peer recognition for the team's research results, but also provided them with a valuable learning and exchange platform.

2024年10月，第32届ACM国际多媒体会议（ACM MM）在澳大利亚墨尔本隆重召开，来自全球的多媒体领域专家学者齐聚一堂，探讨最新的研究进展与技术突破。实验室喻莉教授与其硕士生石俊杰受邀出席本次会议，展示了其团队在多媒体和计算机视觉领域的最新研究成果，受到与会专家的高度关注和赞赏。石俊杰硕士生作为志愿者和演讲者双重身份参与本次会议，对他来说是学术旅途中的一个重要里程碑。他分享道，参加ACM MM不仅为他提供了一个学习与交流的独特平台，还为他个人成长带来了诸多宝贵的机会。作为会议志愿者，石俊杰全程参与了会议的组织工作，积极协助参会流程，和组织者、工作人员以及参会者进行了密切互动。志愿服务的经历不仅增强了他对会议运作的理解，也让他更加深入地融入了国际研究社区。在口头报告环节中，石俊杰首次向全球顶尖学者展示了自己的研究成果，既兴奋又紧张。然而，在浓厚的学术氛围中，他逐渐由不安转为自信。听众们提出了富有建设性的反馈意见，并激发了他对研究的进一步思考，促使他在未来的工作中继续探索和改进。他表示，非常感谢ACM MM会议组织者给予的宝贵机会，让他得以在国际舞台上展示自己的研究，并拓展了在多媒体领域的学术视野。喻莉教授对团队成员的表现表示赞赏，认为本次ACM MM之行不仅让团队的研究成果得到了国际同行的认可，更为他们提供了一个宝贵的学习和交流平台。
