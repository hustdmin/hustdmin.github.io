  Today, Professor Yu Li, head of our lab, was invited to the School of Computer and Information Science at Chongqing Normal University to give an academic report on 'Image and Video Coding Technology for Machine Vision.' The report analyzed the main proposals of the current international standard VCM and introduced the main progress of current machine vision coding research. The faculty and students present said they gained a great deal.

今天，我实验室负责人喻莉教授受邀至重庆师范大学计算机与信息科学学院做关于“面向机器视觉的图像视频编码技术”的学术报告。该报告将分析目前国际标准VCM的主要提案，介绍当前机器视觉编码研究的主要进展。在场师生表示收获很大。
